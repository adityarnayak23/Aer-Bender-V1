STEP 1

How to Hold the Flute

Hold flute sideways to your right. Left wrist turns inward; Right hand reaches forward.

LEFT HAND PALM FACES YOU

Turn left wrist inward towards your face so index, middle, and ring curve over Holes 1, 2, and 3.

Left Pinky: Free in the air (relaxed, not on flute)
RIGHT HAND PALM FACES FORWARD

Keep right hand facing forward naturally (away from you). 4 fingers rest over Holes 4, 5, 6, and 7.

Right Hand: Uses Index, Middle, Ring & Pinky

Blow gently across the blow hole (◎) like blowing across the top of a bottle.
