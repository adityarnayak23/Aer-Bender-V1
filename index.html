HOW TO PLAY

Learn to Play Aer Bender

Hold hands up to the flute • 2 fingers for Sa • See all notes below

1. WHICH HAND

Left Hand Top, Right Hand Down

✋ LEFT HAND Holes 1, 2, 3 (Near Mouth)
Index (1) Middle (2) Ring (3)
➔
🤚 RIGHT HAND Holes 4, 5, 6, 7 (Tube Body)
Index (4) Middle (5) Ring (6) Pinky (7)

Blow air at the circle (◎) with your lips and place fingers over the 7 holes.

2. START PLAYING

2 Fingers Down = SA (Saw)

Cover Holes 1 & 2 with your left index & middle finger to immediately sound the fundamental note Sa (C4)!

3. VISUAL ANIMATED SCALE

How to Play Sa, Ri, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni

SA (C4) Cover first 2 holes with Left Index & Middle finger
