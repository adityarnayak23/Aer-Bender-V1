Learn to Play Aer Bender

Step 1 of 4 • Hand Posture & Finger Placement

STEP 1

How to Hold the Flute

Hold flute sideways to your right. Left palm faces you, right palm faces forward.

Blow gently across the blow hole (◎) like blowing across the top of a bottle.

STEP 2

Wake Up the Flute with Sa

Cover Holes 1 & 2 with your left hand to wake up the flute and play Sa.

STEP 3

How to Play All 7 Notes
